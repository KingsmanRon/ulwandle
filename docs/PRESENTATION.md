## Slide 2: The Problem
### South Africa's Water Crisis

**Key Challenges:**
- 📊 **High Water Wastage**: 15-35% Non-Revenue Water (NRW) across metros
- 🚰 **Infrastructure Issues**: Aging pipes, leaks, burst mains
- 📉 **Water Stress**: Per capita usage below WHO recommendations
- ⚠️ **Manual Operations**: Water shutdowns require manual intervention
- 📋 **Data Fragmentation**: No unified monitoring system across metros

**Impact:**
- Billions of liters lost annually
- Service disruptions affecting millions
- Financial losses for municipalities
- Public health concerns

## Slide 10: Data Integrity - Blockchain
### Cryptographic Verification

**Why Blockchain?**
- Ensures data hasn't been tampered with
- Creates immutable audit trail
- Meets World Bank PforR compliance requirements
- Builds stakeholder trust

**How It Works:**
1. Water data is generated
2. SHA-256 hash created
3. Blockchain block formed
4. Hash stored with data
5. Chain verified on demand

**Benefits:**
- Data integrity guarantee
- Compliance ready
- Transparent verification
- Audit trail for inspections
